How to submit sitemap.xml to Google Search Console

1. Open Google Search Console and select your property (domain or URL-prefix).
2. On the left menu choose "Sitemaps".
3. Under "Add a new sitemap" enter: `sitemap.xml` (or full path `https://www.shreeharichasmaghar.com/sitemap.xml`).
4. Click `Submit`.
5. After submission, refresh the Sitemaps page and check for "Last read" and status. If errors appear, click the sitemap name to inspect details.

Tips for validation & reindexing
- Use URL Inspection for individual pages showing errors in Search Console; click "Request indexing" after the page is live and shows "URL is available to Google".
- For large fixes, use the "Validate Fix" button on an issue in the Coverage or Pages report.
- Keep `robots.txt` accessible at `https://www.shreeharichasmaghar.com/robots.txt` and ensure it does not block `sitemap.xml`.
- After you make domain/redirect changes, re-submit sitemap and request indexing for top pages.
